| ATTR-ENG-05 | done:ITER-0003 | `loop_restart=true` iteratively re-enters the selected target under a pairwise-unique fresh log root without reparsing the prepared graph or recursive driver growth ([§2.7](../../../upstream/strongdm-attractor/attractor-spec.md#27-edge-attributes), [§3.2](../../../upstream/strongdm-attractor/attractor-spec.md#32-core-execution-loop)). `SCN-LOOP-RESTART` proves the exact `pipeline.restarting` map precedes the next `pipeline.started` and target `stage.started`, the selected edge remains the target incoming edge for fidelity/thread resolution, and the same graph, custom registration, cancellation callable, event sink, context atom, nested semantic values, and accumulated logs cross two rapid restarts. The low-level positive-integer `:max-steps` defaults to 10,000 and bounds the whole invocation: one step is one nonterminal handler execution or one goal-gate reroute, while recognizing a satisfied terminal is free. Exact-limit terminal success, pre-handler exhaustion, exhausted unsatisfied-gate behavior, the exact failure reason/event, and invalid-option rejection are covered. Restart closes and clears every departed fidelity map, attempting every close callback; cleanup preserves a primary execution error and otherwise throws the first cleanup error after all attempts. Real full-fidelity evidence proves three distinct session histories and exactly one close per segment. |
